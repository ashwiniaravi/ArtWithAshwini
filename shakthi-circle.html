What is Shakthi Circle?

One skill a month, just for you.

Shakthi Circle is a self-love activity — a small, trusted WhatsApp space where women of all ages come together. Daughters, mothers, grandmothers, great-grandmothers. One simple skill each month, chosen just for you.

No pressure. No checking in. You do it when you are ready, how you are ready. Even one skill in a whole year, done with full attention and real feeling, is enough.

If you ever need help with a skill, just reach out. This is a trusted space — open, warm, and without judgment.

“Every moment is a fresh beginning.”

Month by month

Skills we’ve explored — and what’s coming

Each month opens a different door — a yoga practice, a memory ritual, a creative act, a return to language. Simple, meaningful, yours.

February 2026 — Skill 1

Self-Care Through Yoga

Gentle yoga routines for body, mind, and energy. Sunrise and sunset practices to start and end the day with care.

March 2026 — Skill 2

Strengthening Memory & Brain Clarity

Recall a memory from your life and write it in detail. A simple private practice for the mind and heart — at your own pace.

April 2026 — Skill 3

Caring for the Earth — Mindful Recycling

Give a second life to what you already have. Transform old items into new keepsakes. Create · Reuse · Be Mindful.

May 2026 — Skill 4

The Power of Language

Slow down, read, reflect, express. Make reading and writing a gentle ritual — in any language you know, at your own pace.

June 2026 — Skill 5

Reconnecting Through Letters

Pause. Write. Connect. Think of someone you miss and let your words travel to them — through a handwritten letter. A gift that can be treasured for years.

July 2026 — Skill 6

Learn a Skill From Another Person

Learn one skill directly from a real person — a family recipe, a craft, a household trick, a few words in another language. If possible, choose someone outside your usual age group, culture, or life experience. Every person is a teacher.

August – February

More to come

Six more skills, announced in the channel each month. Every one chosen to nourish, strengthen, and inspire.
