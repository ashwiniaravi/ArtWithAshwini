My Journey

I've always been a teacher — of visual art, dance, craft, and even jewelry making with friends over the years. My curiosity keeps circling back to Kalamkari, a tradition I return to again and again, even as I wander into new ones like Madhubani and Phad.

I'm always discovering something new, and I love sharing what I learn with more people along the way — that's the whole point.

Ten years of teaching and art — and I'm just getting started.

One day, I'd love to bring it all together in a showcase — my own work, and the work of everyone I've taught over the years.
