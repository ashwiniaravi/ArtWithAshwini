Curriculum & lesson design

Art lessons built around the learner

I design learning systems and lesson kits for folk art and beyond — structured so that anyone can follow them, in a group class or one on one. Good curriculum makes art accessible and joyful for every learner.

My approach

Art for everyone — really

I believe any person, at any age, with any background, can experience the joy and healing of making art. That belief drives every lesson plan I write.

Good curriculum doesn’t just teach a technique. It gives the learner context — the story behind the art, the culture that created it, the materials and why they matter. It meets people where they are and invites them further in.

I have designed lessons for kids from 3rd grade up, for adults who have never drawn before, and for students studying Indian folk art traditions at an advanced level. I also sell printable art lesson kits for kids of all ages — available on Etsy.

What I design

Programs & lesson types

Kids — All Ages

Art lesson kits for kids of all ages — elements of art, artist study, techniques, and creative thinking. Printable PDFs available on Etsy.

Elementary School

Lessons designed for school kids to explore the work of a real artist — their style, their story, and their techniques. Every lesson builds curiosity, creativity, and a love of making.

Middle & High School

Technique deepening, art history connection, creative projects with room for personal voice. Designed to meet students who are starting to form an artistic identity.

Adult Programs

Structured folk art learning across three levels. Also workshops, meditative art, and collaborative projects for adults of all backgrounds.

One of the things I do

Docent work in schools

I also bring art into school classrooms as a trained art docent — taking students inside the world of a real artist, looking together, discussing, and making something that connects. I have worked with elementary students across Maple Valley and surrounding schools.

If you are looking for a docent presenter for your school or classroom, feel free to reach out.
