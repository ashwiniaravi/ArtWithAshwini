Indian Folk Art Program

A dedicated path into six Indian folk art traditions, taught directly by a practicing artist — Kalamkari, Madhubani, Patachitra, Phad, Pichwai, and Gond. Each tradition carries its own grammar: its own symbols, patterns, and stories.

Open to teens (with a parent) and adults learning side by side. Three levels let you go as deep as you want — start with no experience, or return to build on what you already know.

✦

Basic — no art experience necessary, learn the fundamentals of each tradition

✦

Intermediate — builds on Basic, deepens technique and detail

✦

Advanced — builds on Basic and Intermediate, for serious practice

✦

Teens welcome alongside a parent · Adults welcome on their own

Why learn with me

How My Class Works

I'm a storyteller first. Painting, teaching, the words I choose for a lesson — all of it is in service of one thing: a good story, told well enough to be understood and kept. That instinct doesn't stop at visual art. Apart from my art path, I love stories and songs just as much — making Tamil and Sanskrit songs feel simple for kids growing up in the U.S., helping them speak Tamil more comfortably, and sing and dance to that same tradition.

I love designing curriculum this way — creating simple lessons that work for beginners and advanced learners alike, a space wide enough for everyone to grow, kids to adults, with art experience or none. I work with century-old Indian folk art traditions and fine art foundations both, and before any art form reaches your child in class, I've already gone deep into it myself, years of learning from real teachers, so it arrives clear, honest, and easy to hold onto. Being an art docent lets me be around children and watch their curiosity and understanding unfold — that feeling never gets old.

Every lesson I plan is about more than just the art — it's about the creative process, and helping each art form take real root in the person learning it. That's why I make room for kids to write and reflect on what they made, not only make it. Children who put words to their art build a richer emotional vocabulary and a deeper connection to what they created — the story becomes theirs to keep and tell.

What your child grows through my class

A well-told story can carry wisdom, joy, and connection further than technique alone — that's what I bring to every class.
