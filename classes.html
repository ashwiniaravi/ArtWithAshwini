Art Classes — Kids & Adults

My classes are built on a structured curriculum that teaches the real foundations of art — elements, principles, tools, and techniques — so students can create their own original work with confidence. Every lesson builds clarity and skill, guiding learners to think, plan, and express ideas the way artists do.

I believe learning art from different parts of the world changes the way we see, think, and create. When students explore Indian art traditions alongside fine art from other cultures, they encounter new visual languages, symbols, and ways of expressing ideas — broadening both their worldview and their creative range.

✦

Foundations — elements and principles of art, vocabulary, tools, and techniques

✦

Original creation — applying learned skills to personal, meaningful projects

✦

Indian art traditions (Kalamkari, Madhubani, Gond, Phad, Patachitra, Pichwai) and world art

✦

Art as thinking — observation, decision-making, and creative problem-solving

✦

One-on-one or small groups · No art experience needed for beginner sessions

Mom & Me · private sessions

Paint & Breathe

You don't need to know what you want to paint — that's exactly where we start. Before your session, we have a short conversation — about what you love, what your child loves, and what story you want to hold on to.

The art — whether folk art, abstract, or something entirely your own — comes from that conversation. Every session is guided, personal, and designed for one thing: the most memorable experience you can have with your child.

One session or a series · All materials included · Fully guided · Pricing on request

Let's create a beautiful memory, cherished for years.
